Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Cellar Social ("we", "our", or "the app") is a private wine community app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Device identifier: A randomly generated UUID is created on first launch and stored on your device. This is used as your account identity. No email address or name is required to use the app.
  • Display name and avatar: Optionally provided by you during setup. Stored in our database and visible to members of your circles.
  • Wine data: Bottle details, tasting notes, and farm visit logs you enter. Stored in our database, visible only to you.
  • Camera images: Photos taken to scan wine labels are processed by Google's Gemini AI to identify the wine. Images are not stored on our servers.
  • Location: Used only to find nearby wine events and detect your local currency. Never stored. Never shared.
  • Subscription status: Managed by Apple via the App Store. We store your subscription plan (monthly or annual) locally on your device.
  • Circle and social data: Messages, forum posts, and gift records you share within your private circles. Visible only to circle members.

2. How we use your data

  • To provide the features of the app (cellar tracking, tastings, circles, events)
  • To identify wine labels using Google Gemini AI
  • To find wine events near your location
  • To allow private sharing within your invite-only circles

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-party services

  • Supabase — our database provider. Data is stored in encrypted, access-controlled servers. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Gemini API — used to identify wine labels and suggest nearby events. Images sent for scanning are processed per Google's privacy terms. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare — our API infrastructure. cloudflare.com/privacypolicy
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles all subscription payments. We never see your payment details.

4. Data retention

Your data is retained as long as you use the app. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us (see Section 6). Deleting the app removes locally stored data; server-side data is deleted upon request.

5. Children

Cellar Social is not directed at children under 17. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

6. Contact & deletion requests

To request deletion of your data or ask any privacy question, email: privacy@cellarsocial.app

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will reflect changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
